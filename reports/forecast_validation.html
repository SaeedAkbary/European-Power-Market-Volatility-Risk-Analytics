Exploratory price-risk backtest

Actual SMARD targets, monthly expanding fits from 2024. D−1 11:00 Berlin cutoff; price features lagged at least 48 hours. Linear quantile model uses a fixed recent 90-day training window; other fits expand. Fixed model settings; quantiles rearranged to prevent crossing. Seasonal baseline uses prior-week price plus training-only delivery-hour residual quantiles. Linear quantile probabilities reuse the seasonal classifier benchmark. Calibration is assessed out of sample, not fitted on test labels. Daily q50 dispersion is a point proxy and excludes conditional residual variance. Source publication/vintage history is unavailable: these are exploratory historical results, NOT a verified executable ex-ante strategy. No fundamental or weather features are used. No claim of statistically significant superiority; uncertainty intervals remain future work.
