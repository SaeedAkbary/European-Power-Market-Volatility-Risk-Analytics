Purchaser, solar and wind exposure

HYPOTHETICAL portfolios: purchaser 1 MW flat; generators follow observed German aggregate solar/wind output scaled to 1 MW at the full-sample observed peak, not installed capacity or an individual plant. Renewable capture price is sum(price × MWh)/sum(MWh); it excludes subsidy, fees, curtailment decisions, imbalance and basis risk. MW × 0.25 gives MWh. Before the MTU change, the known hourly contract applies to its four quarters; afterward genuine quarter-hour prices are used. No missing prices are forward-filled beyond contract end. Reference 100 EUR/MWh: generation P&L=(spot-reference)×MWh; purchaser reverses sign. Loss=-P&L. Scenarios are complete local delivery days (23/24/25 hours), so horizon varies on DST days. Regime is the first-hour descriptive state, including calibration-year states. VaR/ES assume historical days are representative; small regime strata imply unstable tails. Equal peak scaling does not equalize energy or commercial risk.
